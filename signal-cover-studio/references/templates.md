# Templates

## editorial-minimal
Calm, premium, concise. This is part of the clean gradient baseline.

## editorial-cover
Magazine-cover typography on the clean gradient baseline. Best for X, Xiaohongshu, or any elegant social card.

## comic-explainer
Speech bubble + badge structure. Best when the user explicitly wants explanatory, lively, more playful Xiaohongshu visuals. Not the default acceptance path.

## wechat-article-clean
Readable long-form article HTML. Keeps the article looking like a designed essay instead of a raw markdown dump.
